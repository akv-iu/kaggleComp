### Recovering destroyed goods: three repairs for the nightly shed discard, and the price wall that refuses all three

- **The leak is real and it was mis-priced twice — see the corrected bullet under "Read the environment source" above for the measurement.** 174 units a game across both farms at `townCenterSellInterval: 24` (85 STRAWBERRY, 33 WHEAT, 26 MILK, 18 FERTILIZER, 12 WOOL), 4 units at interval 12. At the interval-24 price table that is nominally ~$13,000 a farm, 13% of the score. All three repairs below were measured on six seeds at both intervals.
- **(a) Any loaded unit goes home.** `KEEP_ITEMS = {WHEAT, GOOSE, COW, SHEEP}`, `DROP_LOAD = 6`, and in `_assign` a still-idle unit carrying that many non-keep items is pulled out of pass 2 and given the dock walk. **-$6,217 at interval 24 and -$7,809 at 12.** A bare threshold fires all day and steals priority-0 water and feed work.
- **(b) The same, retimed to the turns this ledger already prices at zero.** `DROP_LOAD = 3` and the trigger `hour + min_distance_to_dock >= 23`, so the unit leaves at exactly the last hour it can still arrive, spending the ~596 moves a game that `_end_of_day` deletes anyway. **-$4,838 at interval 24 and -$4,733 at 12**, and the mechanism fires precisely as designed: on seed 0 at interval 24 **discards fall 174 -> 52 and strawberry discards 85 -> 27** while that game's score falls $7,688. **This also closes the pairing the dusk entry left open** — "a productive job for the freed carrier" was found, and it still loses.
- **Why (a) and (b) both fail, and this is the run's most transferable number: a leak measured in *units* is not a leak measured in *money* whenever the product is near its own saturation point.** Strawberry's `above_func` is linear with amp **$1.92 a unit**, and our interval-24 mirror ends **113 units below `I0`**. Recovering 85 strawberry a farm puts **170** units into that market, crosses `I0`, and takes the quote from $209 to roughly **$11**. Every product this farm sells except EGG is in the same position. Against a single real opponent who does *not* fix the same leak the arithmetic is different — our 85 units alone move strawberry from -113 to -28, roughly $209 -> $164, a net gain near $6,500 — but **the mirror cannot express a one-sided supply increase and no local instrument this project owns can.** Same wall as `BERRY_TILES = 48`.
- **(c) The repair that costs no unit-turns is positive at both intervals and still cannot be shipped.** The shed cap is enforced against shed *plus* everything the crew holds, so the dump trigger should be too. One line, with `carried` already a parameter of `_market`: `pressure = shed_used > SHED_PRESSURE or shed_used + sum(carried.values()) > SHED_CAP`. Six-seed mirror **+$1,759 at interval 24 and +$1,126 at 12 — the only candidate this run positive at both.** Firing earlier is worse where it counts: threshold `SHED_CAP - 15` gives +$1,124 / +$1,159 and `SHED_CAP - 25` gives +$992 / +$884, and both are **negative on seeds 0-3 at interval 12** (-$1,129 and -$2,098) where the plain form is +$28. The full gate returned **3/8, 2 exact ties, mean +$34.0, mirror +$28.2**, every status DONE, with **six of eight pairings perfectly antisymmetric** (+34/-34, 0/0, +189/-189) — candidate and baseline playing the same game. The rule never fires on seeds 0-3 at interval 12, because that is the configuration in which the leak does not exist.
- **Test again only with:** a gate at `townCenterSellInterval: 24`, or a head-to-head sample large enough to resolve $1,000 against a per-seed SD of $7,900. (a) and (b) are closed outright — any repair that spends labour to recover saturated goods pays twice and collects once. (c) is correct and unshippable; it is the fifth candidate in this file that is positive at both intervals and null on the gate's four seeds.

### The opening is cash-bound, not tile-bound and not labour-bound (carrot priced and ruled out without a benchmark)

- **Measured 2026-08-17 on a seed-0 mirror at interval 24, and it settles what the opening idleness is.** PASS by day: **7, 12, 83, 74, 68, 52, 53, 88, 58, 58** on days 0-9 and **0-14 a day from day 10 to day 28**, with 58 on day 29 — **534 of the game's 714 PASS unit-turns live on days 2-9**, at 27-49% of those days' unit-turns, and at hours 18-22 on those days there are only **2-5 jobs standing on the board for 7-9 units**. Beside it, the day-by-day census: **12 of the 25 opening tiles carry nothing from day 2 to day 5**, money at dawn runs **$238 / $452 / $464 / $731 / $973 / $697 / $829** on days 2-8, and there are **zero bare tiles on days 7-9**.
- **So the idle crew is not the constraint and neither are the tiles.** Every filler has now been refused: opening wheat (1/4, -$4,361), tomato (-$2,720 / -$2,836), melon as the whole opening (0/4, -$10,227). The last unplanted candidate, **CARROT**, was priced this run and ruled out **without spending a benchmark**, which is the cheapest refusal in this file. It is the best-shaped crop in `CROPS` for the job — $20 seed, harvest at age 3, 3 units unfertilized, and a `log` below-curve so flat that 100 extra units move the quote **$0.40 in total** (against tomato's $6 and strawberry's $192), so it is the one crop the mirror's supply penalty cannot touch. It fails on cash alone: **`budget = money - CASH_FLOOR - n_animals * keep` is negative on days 2-5**, so a $20 seed cannot be funded without raiding the feed reserve that stops the herd walking off, and on the first day it *can* be funded (day 6, budget ~$197) the berry field has already taken every bare tile.
- **How to apply.** Stop proposing work for the idle opening crew. The opening's binding constraint is the bank on days 2-7, the same one the herd rush is already fighting, and the only lever on it is a cash crop that lands before day 8.
- **Reconsider if:** something else frees day-2-to-7 cash. Then carrot is the first thing to try, and it should be planted **last** in the `_scan` empty-tile loop and bought **last** in `_market`, so it can only ever take a tile and a dollar that nothing else wanted.
