### The nightly shed discard is a real leak at the competition's demand and a rounding error at the benchmark's

- **Measured 2026-08-17 and it corrects an entry in the worked section above.** `_drop_inventories_to_shed` empties every unit's pack into the shed at nightfall and **silently discards whatever does not fit**. This ledger recorded "4 units discarded, all milk - the shed is not a leak, stop suspecting it". That measurement was taken at `townCenterSellInterval: 12`. Patched across a seed-0 mirror at **interval 24 the same game destroys 174 units across both farms - 85 STRAWBERRY, 33 WHEAT, 26 MILK, 18 FERTILIZER, 12 WOOL - about 87 units a farm**, on nights 19, 20, 21, 23, 24, 25 and 27. At interval 12: **4 units**. Both numbers reproduce.
- **The mechanism, exactly, and neither half is the shed being full.** At dusk the shed holds **29-49** of 100, so there is 51-71 units of room; the crew is holding **76-83**. It is the *crew* that is over capacity. Produce rides in a pack until its unit goes idle, and the idle-drop branch in `_assign` is the only thing that ever walks it home - but **PASS is 0-13 unit-turns a day from day 20 onward** (against 52-88 a day on days 2-9), so from day 19 nothing is ever idle and every harvest waits for the nightly dump.
- **What the shed floor is made of.** Wool sits at **27-41 units every day from day 14**, because at interval 24 wool quotes $1-55 against its $130 floor and only the pressure branch can move it; wheat holds 17-36 as the feed reserve. That is the 45-55 units of permanent occupancy which leaves the crew too little room.
- **Two repairs that spend unit-turns both lose, and the mechanism fires exactly as designed.** (a) Any unit carrying >= 6 non-keep items is pulled out of pass 2 and sent to a dock: **-$6,217 at interval 24 and -$7,809 at 12**, six seeds a cell. (b) The same, retimed to cost only the turns the ledger already prices at zero - a unit hauls when `hour + distance-to-nearest-dock >= 23`, i.e. at exactly the last hour it can still arrive, spending the ~596 moves a game that `_end_of_day` deletes anyway: **-$4,838 / -$4,733**, and on seed 0 at interval 24 **discards fall 174 -> 52 and strawberry discards 85 -> 27** while the score falls $7,688. So the units are recovered and the money is not.
- **Why, and it is the most useful number in this entry: the recovered goods are worth a small fraction of their quoted price.** Strawberry's `above_func` is `linear` with amp **$1.92 a unit**, and our interval-24 mirror ends **113 units below `I0`**. Recovering 85 strawberry a farm puts **170 units** into that market, which crosses `I0` and takes the quote from $209 to roughly $11. **A leak measured in units is not a leak measured in money whenever the product is near its own saturation point**, and every product this farm sells except EGG is. Against a single real opponent who does *not* fix the same leak the arithmetic is different - our 85 units alone move strawberry from -113 to -28, roughly $209 -> $164, for a net gain around $6,500 - but the mirror cannot express a one-sided supply increase and neither can any local instrument this project has.
- **The repair that costs no unit-turns is positive at both intervals.** `pressure` in `_market` is computed off `shed_used` alone; the cap is enforced against `shed + everything the crew is holding`, and `carried` is already a parameter. One line: `pressure = shed_used > SHED_PRESSURE or shed_used + sum(carried.values()) > SHED_CAP`. Six-seed mirror: **+$1,759 at interval 24 and +$1,126 at 12**. Adding a margin so it fires earlier makes it *worse* on the seeds the gate uses: threshold `SHED_CAP - 15` gives +$1,124 / +$1,159 and `SHED_CAP - 25` gives +$992 / +$884, and both go negative on seeds 0-3 at interval 12 (-$1,129 and -$2,098) where the plain form is +$28.
- **Test again only with:** a gate that can score a one-sided supply increase. The haul family is closed - two repairs, both of which recovered the units and lost the money, and the price wall that explains them is the same one that refuses `BERRY_TILES = 48`.
