### The competition draws its shop unlocks *with* replacement; the installed environment draws *without*

- **Discovered 2026-08-17, from the `town` block of nine replays, and it is the second configuration gap between the benchmark and the competition.** `_end_of_day` in
  `.venv/.../kaggriculture.py` reads `remaining = [s for s in SHOPS if s not in town["unlocked_shops"]]` and draws from that, so a local game unlocks **all eight distinct shops** across days 3-24. Every competition replay unlocks **eight times from the full set of eight**, duplicates included: `episode-91992026` opens ICE_CREAM_SHOP on day 3 *and again on day 9*, YARN_STORE on days 6 and 15, SMOOTHIE_SHOP on 12 and 21, FARMERS_MARKET on 18 and 24 — four distinct shops, each doubled. `episode-92762105` draws SMOOTHIE_SHOP three times.
- **The statistics match sampling with replacement exactly.** Distinct shops per game over nine replays: 4, 5, 5, 5, 5, 5, 6, 6, 7 — **mean 5.33 against a theoretical `8*(1-(7/8)^8) = 5.25`**, where the local environment gives 8 every time. `main.py` against itself locally opens all eight at both intervals.
- **What it changes, and what it does not.** The *expected* number of shop-listings per product is unchanged (8 draws x P(shop lists it)), so this is a pure variance and concentration effect, not a level shift. What it changes is the tail: `P(a given shop never opens) = (7/8)^8 = 34.4%`, and a duplicated shop drains at **double rate for the rest of the season**. **WOOL's only shop is YARN_STORE, so a third of all competition games have no wool drain at all** - observed in 3 of 9 replays, and wool ends at $1 in every one of them. STRAWBERRY has four shops, so it goes undrained only 0.4% of the time, but its drain rate ranges from 1x to 3x.
- **Why this matters more than it looks: it is the reason the shop-conditioned screens came out null.** The 2026-08-15 shop-mix screen was **bit-identical to baseline on five of six seeds**, and that was read as "there is no herd decision left open once the lottery is legible". The real reason is simpler: **locally there is nothing to condition on, because every shop eventually opens.** A rule that says "do not stack the species whose shop has not appeared" is nearly a no-op in an environment where the shop is guaranteed to appear, and is a live one-in-three bet in the competition.
- **How to apply.** Treat the local shop draw the way this ledger already treats `townCenterSellInterval`: it is a *harness* property. Any candidate that reads `obs["town"]["unlocked_shops"]` will be under-measured by the benchmark, in the same direction and for the same reason. There is no way to move the gate, so record the mechanism and prefer hypotheses that do not depend on it.
- **Required pairing:** nothing. Like reading the environment source, it is free.
- **Reconsider if:** the installed `kaggle_environments` is ever updated to match. Check by running one local episode and counting `len(set(unlocked_shops))` against `len(unlocked_shops)`; they are equal locally today and unequal in every competition replay.
