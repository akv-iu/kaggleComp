### The benchmark is not the competition, part two: the competition draws its shops *with* replacement

- **Discovered 2026-08-17 from the `town` block of nine replays, and it sits beside the `townCenterSellInterval` entry above as the second harness/competition gap.** `_end_of_day` in the installed environment reads `remaining = [s for s in SHOPS if s not in town["unlocked_shops"]]` and draws from that, so **a local game unlocks all eight distinct shops** across days 3-24. Every competition replay unlocks **eight times from the full set of eight, duplicates included**: `episode-91992026` opens ICE_CREAM_SHOP on day 3 *and again on day 9*, YARN_STORE on 6 and 15, SMOOTHIE_SHOP on 12 and 21, FARMERS_MARKET on 18 and 24 — four distinct shops, every one doubled. `episode-92762105` draws SMOOTHIE_SHOP three times.
- **The statistics match sampling with replacement exactly.** Distinct shops per game over nine replays: 4, 5, 5, 5, 5, 5, 6, 6, 7 — **mean 5.33 against the theoretical `8*(1-(7/8)^8) = 5.25`**, where the local environment gives 8 every time. Verified by running one local mirror at both intervals: all eight, no duplicates.
- **What it changes and what it does not.** The *expected* number of shop-listings per product is unchanged (eight draws x P(a shop lists it)), so this is a pure variance and concentration effect, not a level shift — do not re-derive average prices from it. What it changes is the tail: **`P(a given shop never opens) = (7/8)^8 = 34.4%`**, and a duplicated shop drains at **double rate for the rest of the season**. **WOOL's only shop is YARN_STORE, so about a third of competition games have no wool drain at all** — 3 of 9 replays, wool ending at $1 in every one. STRAWBERRY has four shops so it goes undrained only 0.4% of the time, but its drain runs 1x to 3x, and that range is most of the distance between our best games and our worst: `episode-91992026` drew ICE_CREAM x2 + SMOOTHIE x2 + FARMERS x2 + YARN x2 — every draw on strawberry or wool — and strawberry ended **293 units below `I0` at $264** with the winner on $175,862, while `episode-92754717` drew BAKERY x2 + PIZZA x2 + ICE_CREAM x2 and strawberry ended **41 units above `I0` at $41**, both farms near $56,000.
- **This is the real reason the shop-conditioned screens came out null, and it corrects the entry under "Fixed cow-heavy herd" below.** That screen was recorded as "bit-identical to baseline on five of six seeds ... there is almost no herd decision left open once the lottery can be read". The simpler explanation: **locally there is nothing to condition on, because every shop is guaranteed to open.** "Do not stack the species whose shop has not appeared" is close to a no-op against that guarantee and a live one-in-three bet in the competition.
- **How to apply.** Treat the local shop draw exactly as this ledger treats `townCenterSellInterval`: a harness property that cannot be moved. **Any candidate that reads `obs["town"]["unlocked_shops"]` will be under-measured by the benchmark, in the same direction and for the same reason**, so prefer hypotheses that do not depend on it — and never spend a benchmark on one that does.
- **Required pairing:** nothing. Like reading the environment source, this is free.
- **Reconsider if:** the installed `kaggle_environments` is ever updated to match. One-line check: run a local episode and compare `len(set(unlocked_shops))` with `len(unlocked_shops)` — equal locally today, unequal in every competition replay.
